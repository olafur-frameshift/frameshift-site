The data environment

Why Danish registry data?

Denmark holds unusually rich longitudinal health data, with comparable registers across the Nordic countries. Together they allow population-based studies across diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions, outcomes, and long-term follow-up.

Nationwide longitudinal cohorts

Study complete or near-complete national populations rather than selected research samples, followed across years or decades.

Linkable health data

Combine diagnoses, procedures, prescriptions, cancer data, mortality, and socioeconomic information across national data sources.

Decision-relevant evidence

Generate evidence on epidemiology, treatment patterns, effectiveness, safety, burden of disease, and external comparators.

Evidence we generate

What we generate.

Registry-based studies that answer clinical, regulatory, and commercial questions at population scale.

01

Disease Epidemiology & Burden

Establish the size and shape of the patient population.

Incidence, prevalence, natural history, survival, disease trajectories, and healthcare utilisation, quantified in a defined national population so the figures describe routine care rather than a trial sample.

02

Treatment Patterns & Patient Journeys

See what actually happens after diagnosis.

Treatment initiation, sequencing, switching, adherence, and persistence, reconstructed from prescription and hospital records to show the pathways patients follow through routine care over years.

03

Target Trial Emulation

Bring trial-level design discipline to real-world data.

We define the clinical trial we would ideally run and emulate its key design elements using longitudinal registry data to estimate comparative treatment effects in routine clinical practice.

Explore Target Trial Emulation →
04

External Control Arms

Give a single-arm study something to compare against.

Real-world comparator and natural-history cohorts built from registry data, with eligibility and follow-up aligned to the trial protocol so the comparison rests on a matched population rather than a convenient one.

05

Post-Authorisation Effectiveness & Safety

Follow a treatment once it reaches routine use.

Long-term effectiveness, safety signals, and healthcare outcomes after launch, using nationwide follow-up that continues well beyond the trial period and covers patients who would never have been enrolled.

06

Trial Feasibility & Data Strategy

Find out whether the question can be answered at all.

Which registers hold the variables you need, how complete they are, what sample size is achievable, and which design is realistic, all established before committing to a full study.

Why Frameshift

Design before data.

Real-world evidence is only as good as the study design. Large registry datasets do not remove the need for careful epidemiology: cohort construction, timing, comparability, confounding, endpoint definition, and estimands are settled before analysis begins.

How we work

Epidemiology before analysis

Bias, timing, and comparability decide whether a registry result means anything. We settle the design questions first, and say plainly what the data can and cannot support.

Direct senior involvement

Projects are led and executed by experienced scientists. You work with the people responsible for the study design and the analysis, not through layers of consulting staff.

End-to-end execution

From feasibility and data strategy through cohort specification, analysis, and reporting. A study taken to a decision-ready output, not an isolated statistical task.

Clinical trials

Where the discipline comes from.

We run trial statistics: design, statistical analysis plans, primary and secondary analyses, reporting, and publication. Our client engagements to date include randomised trials, and that work is where the standards behind everything else on this page were set.

Prespecification is not optional

Estimands, endpoints, analysis populations, and the analysis strategy are settled before the data are examined. We hold registry studies to the same rule.

Comparability is designed, not repaired

A trial earns comparable arms by construction. An observational study has to build it deliberately, which is exactly what target trial emulation makes explicit.

Both, on the same project

Trial and registry work often answer neighbouring questions for the same programme. We take on either, and say which one the question actually needs.
