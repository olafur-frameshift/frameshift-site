Why Frameshift

Design before data.

No sample size fixes a cohort defined wrong, a time zero that leaks information from the future, or an endpoint chosen after someone has seen the curves.

  • Prespecified

    The analysis plan is signed off before outcome data are examined.

  • Checked

    Primary results are checked by a second team member.

  • One owner

    Whoever designs the study runs the analysis and writes the methods.

  • To manuscript

    Tables, figures and methods text, ready for submission.
